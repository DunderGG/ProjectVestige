# Phase 5 - Late: What happens next belongs to the harmed people

> **Spoilers.** These drafts keep safety, care, records, investigation and accountability as distinct choices. Use only the branches the affected person has authorized.

## R-01 - Ask what help is wanted

**Placement:** After the survivor has had time and immediate safety is addressed.
**Knowledge:** The survivor has not agreed to care, testimony, recordkeeping, disclosure or confrontation unless they explicitly do so here.

**PLAYER:** I don't want to guess. What would help right now?

**SURVIVOR:** A medic can look at the equipment. Not me. No questions about my body.

**PLAYER:** All right. I'll tell them. Do you want me there?

**SURVIVOR:** Not yet.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Arrange only the requested care]** "I'll tell the medic: equipment only, no examination or questions. I'll wait outside."
  - **SURVIVOR:** Good. Make sure they hear that before they come. I'll stop the visit if I need to.
- **[Offer privacy]** "I can leave you alone. You can call if you want me."
  - **SURVIVOR:** Do that. Don't send someone else to check on me.
- **[Offer a quieter room]** "There's a quieter room down the hall. Want to look at it?"
  - **SURVIVOR:** I'll look first. I won't stay unless I decide to.

**PLAYER:** All right.

## R-02 - Records on the survivor's terms

**Placement:** Only if a survivor chooses to discuss a record or account with Cleo.
**Speakers:** PLAYER, SURVIVOR, CLEO.
**Knowledge:** Permission to speak is not permission to record, keep, copy, or share. Each requires its own choice.

**PLAYER:** Cleo can listen, if you want. I can stay or step outside. You can also stop here.

**SURVIVOR - CHOICES**

- **[Speak with Cleo alone]** "I want to speak with Cleo alone."
  - **PLAYER:** I'll wait outside. I won't ask Cleo what you said.
  - **Production note:** Continue with Cleo and the survivor only.
- **[Allow the player to stay]** "You can stay. Don't answer for me."
  - **PLAYER:** I won't.
  - **Production note:** Continue with all three characters.
- **[Stop before talking]** "I don't want to talk today."
  - **CLEO:** All right. We can leave it there.
  - **Production note:** End the scene immediately. Do not ask again, take notes, or arrange another contact unless invited.

**SURVIVOR:** The researchers used to stand behind the glass with clipboards. Every time I coughed, they made a check mark.

**CLEO:** My notebook is shut. I haven't written that down or made a recording. Do you want to keep talking without a record, ask me to write a short account for you to review, or stop?

**SURVIVOR:** And who gets to read it?

**CLEO:** No one. I haven't made a record. If you ask me to write one, deciding whether I keep it—and whether anyone else sees it—will be separate choices.

**SURVIVOR - CHOICES**

- **[Talk without a record]** "I want to talk, but I don't want it written down."
  - **CLEO:** Understood. The notebook stays shut. I won't take notes or make a copy.
  - **SURVIVOR:** Then just listen. I don't want to explain it twice.
  - **Production note:** Continue only as a private conversation. No transcript, notes, recording, or implied record is created. End when the survivor says they are done; do not ask them to approve or store anything.
- **[Allow a transcript]** "Write only what I choose to tell you. Read it back before you keep anything."
  - **CLEO:** I will. Writing it down doesn't give me permission to keep or share it.
  - **Production note:** Continue to the read-back and separate retention choice below.
- **[Stop for now]** "No. I don't want to continue."
  - **CLEO:** All right. We stop here.
  - **Production note:** End the scene immediately. Do not ask who should stay, ask another question, write anything, or arrange another contact unless invited.

**IF TRANSCRIPT CHOSEN**

**SURVIVOR:** Write this: "They watched from behind the glass and marked each time I coughed." No name, no medical details.

**CLEO:** I'll read it back exactly: "They watched from behind the glass and marked each time I coughed." Is that what you want on the page?

**SURVIVOR - CHOICES**

- **[Approve the exact wording]** "That's right."
- **[Correct the wording]** "Change it to: 'They watched from behind the glass and marked my coughs.' Read it back again."
  - **CLEO:** "They watched from behind the glass and marked my coughs." Is that right?
  - **Production note:** Return to the wording choices. Repeat until the survivor approves the exact text or chooses to destroy it.
- **[Destroy the draft]** "No. Tear it up. I don't want a copy kept."
  - **CLEO:** I will.
  - **Production note:** Show Cleo destroying the only page without copying or recording it. End the scene; do not ask the survivor to reconsider.

**IF THE SURVIVOR APPROVES THE WORDING**

**CLEO:** The words are yours. You can take the page, ask me to keep it sealed until you ask for it back, or have me destroy it now. I won't copy it or share it.

**SURVIVOR - CHOICES**

- **[Keep the page]** "Give it to me. I'll decide what to do with it."
  - **CLEO:** Of course.
- **[Ask Cleo to hold it]** "Keep it sealed in your folio until I ask for it back. Don't copy or show it to anyone."
  - **CLEO:** I will. I won't send it to the Archive unless you make that choice separately.
- **[Destroy the page]** "Destroy it. I don't want a copy kept."
  - **CLEO:** I will.
  - **Production note:** Show Cleo destroying the only page without copying or recording it.

**Production note:** The only record in this scene is the single handwritten page in the transcript branch. It is not testimony for the Archive. Any later decision to share it requires a new, specific request and approval of the exact material and audience. No-record and stop branches never reach transcript review, storage, or sharing.

## R-03 - Specific accountability

**Placement:** Investigation or confrontation is discussed only after evidence supports a specific lead and the affected person chooses whether to proceed.
**Speakers:** PLAYER, SURVIVOR, GAGE.
**Knowledge:** No one is accused solely for rank, faction membership or presence at a hub.

**GAGE:** I tracked the serial stamp on the restraint collars. Leads to a field forge out of Iron Ridge. There's a master sapper's punch mark on the hinge.

**SURVIVOR:** Are you going to drag me in front of them?

**PLAYER:** Nobody is dragging you anywhere. You never have to lay eyes on their uniforms again.

**SURVIVOR:** I don't want to see their faces. I just want their forge fires put out so they never cast another collar.

**GAGE:** Understood. The Bastion elders respect steel and stamped manifests. I take the batch records to the engineering tribunal myself. Your name stays off the docket. You don't owe them a single breath of testimony.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Support a private process]** "We'll report the batch numbers and logs without putting your name on the docket."
  - **SURVIVOR:** Yes. Don't promise me what the elders will decide. Just take away their hammers.
- **[Ask about public accountability]** "Would you want the town to know what was built out there?"
  - **SURVIVOR:** Not today. Don't bring it up again unless I ask.
- **[Pause the process]** "We can lock the evidence in the forge safe and hold off."
  - **SURVIVOR:** Yes. Let the dust settle first.

**GAGE [IF PRIVATE PROCESS]:** I'll submit the manifests to high command. I can't promise how the tribunal rules, but your name won't be on the paper.

**GAGE [IF PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY]:** We keep the files under lock and key. I won't bring public reports up again.

**GAGE [IF THE PROCESS IS PAUSED]:** The manifests stay sealed in my iron chest. No reports until you give the word.

## R-04 - Wellspring decides its own limits

**Placement:** The assembly considers what the town will share or protect after learning of the institute.
**Speakers:** PLAYER, TOWN ADMINISTRATOR, KEEPER OPERATOR, CLEO.
**Knowledge:** Wellspring chooses its own disclosure and risk. The sleeper does not speak for the town or the survivor.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** The assembly can share the institute's location, keep it private, or release a limited report. It has not decided.

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** If the route goes public, people may come here looking for supplies or answers. We need to know what we can safely handle.

**CLEO:** I can separate the town's records from the institute evidence. The Archive does not need Wellspring's cistern map to review the trial files.

**PLAYER:** The survivor hasn't agreed to have their story shared.

**CLEO:** Then their story is not part of this proposal.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** The assembly can decide what Wellspring shares. It cannot decide what the survivor shares.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Support a limited report]** "Share what the institute records establish. Keep the town's locations and the survivor's identity private."
  - **TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** I'll put that wording to the assembly. It still has to decide.
- **[Support a pause]** "Wait until the town has a safety plan."
  - **KEEPER OPERATOR:** That gives us time to secure the route without destroying the evidence.
- **[Decline to advise]** "This is Wellspring's decision. I won't choose for the assembly."
  - **TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** A useful distinction. I'll make it clear that you did not speak for us.

## R-06 - The route continues by choice

**Placement:** A final route scene once the town has a clearer risk boundary and the nomad can choose whether to continue their circuit.
**Knowledge:** The nomad's decision is theirs, not the player's. This is about consent and risk, not gratitude.

**NOMAD:** I've got the trail cairns marked. The route is still open if Wellspring wants trade.

**PLAYER:** And if you don't want to keep the route open?

**NOMAD:** Then I kick dust over the markers and take the long loop around the salt pans. A runner who forces their way into a jittery valley doesn't get a medal—they get ambushed by scared folk with pitchforks. Out here, knowing when to pack your mule and walk away is how you live to see next spring.

**PLAYER:** Wellspring worries that an open trail brings trouble to their doorstep.

**NOMAD:** Of course they do! They've got cabbage patches and kids sleeping in dry beds; I'd be suspicious of strangers in dust goggles too. That's why we don't force it. The trail will still be there when the dust settles.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Respect the nomad's decision]** "Then pack your gear when you need to. No one will blame you."
  - **NOMAD:** Thanks, sleeper. It's good to trade with someone who knows the difference between a trail partner and a prison guard.
- **[Ask what would make it safer]** "What would make a route stop feel safer to you?"
  - **NOMAD:** Clear signal stones by the gate, dry straw in the barn, and one person who doesn't panic when an unfamiliar face comes down the ridge.
- **[Offer to help with warnings]** "I can help spot storm clouds and keep the water barrels full."
  - **NOMAD:** Now that's music to a walker's ears. Keep the well clean and I'll keep the salt coming.
