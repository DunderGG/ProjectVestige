# Phase 4 - Middle: The trials become undeniable

> **Spoilers.** The institute establishes the abusive Continuance Trials without making either faction collectively guilty or turning a survivor into evidence.

## M-01 - The institute

**Placement:** The main story reaches the old research institute. The player finds corroborating records from separate programs.
**Knowledge:** The records establish that the Index's cognitive integrations and Iron Creed frame conversions happened. They do not establish one shared command, every member's knowledge, or every individual's responsibility.

**PLAYER:** This is not just a stack of damaged clinic files. There are intake forms, procedure logs and people marked "transferred" with no destination.

**CLEO:** The dates line up with what I found. That is not a clerical error. But we still do not know what happened to each person.

**GAGE:** The other wing has frame-conversion notes. The equipment request bears the Creed's name. That tells us who supplied the actuators, not who in the Bastion knew what they were being bolted to.

**PLAYER:** They did all of this here?

**CLEO:** The records put both projects under this roof. Two separate programs running parallel lines.

**GAGE:** They called it 'system readiness.' That's sapper talk for welding the hatches shut so the crew can't run when the bulkhead groans. It's butchery.

**PLAYER:** They trapped them.

**CLEO:** They treated living minds like surplus components to be slotted into a rack. Look at these intake logs... after day three, they stopped using names entirely. Just accession codes.

**GAGE:** And that hydraulic manifold on the floor is Bastion stock. My own guild stamped the requisition. Before we start pointing rifles, I want the signatures on the maintenance log. Every bolt has someone's mark on it.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Prioritize finding the person]** "Can we reach the person being kept here safely?"
  - **GAGE:** Check your footing and watch the door frame. You don't rush into an unbraced room blind.
- **[Ask what the records establish]** "Let's separate what we know from what we suspect."
  - **CLEO:** The physical logs prove the machinery was installed and operated here. They don't prove every engineer in the valley was complicit. We document what's bolted to the floor before we accuse the world.
- **[Ask for a pause]** "I need a moment before we go further."
  - **CLEO:** Take it. The stone won't crumble and the paper won't burn while you catch your breath.

## M-02 - First contact with the living exhibit

**Placement:** The player reaches the person displayed as the Index's "living exhibit." Keep the scene calm and allow distance.
**Knowledge:** Their identity, condition, history and wishes are not established by this discovery. The player can learn the display is not voluntary without demanding testimony.

**PLAYER:** I'm not here to hurt you. I'm stepping back to the doorway.

**LIVING EXHIBIT:** Are you here to move me somewhere else?

**PLAYER:** No. I'm not touching any levers or moving you anywhere. If seeing me makes your chest tight, I can walk out right now.

**LIVING EXHIBIT:** I don't know. The door's standing open, but they built this glass cage. People stood out there and took notes while I choked on the breathing tube.

**PLAYER:** Nobody is taking notes now. We can stay back, leave, or just give you room to breathe.

**LIVING EXHIBIT:** Who else is out in the corridor?

**PLAYER:** Cleo from the Archive and Gage from the forge. They're waiting by the stairs. They won't come in unless you ask.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Offer immediate practical help]** "Can I leave a canteen of fresh water on the bench for you?"
  - **LIVING EXHIBIT:** Water. Put it down and step back. Don't come near the housing.
- **[Offer privacy]** "I'll wait out in the hall. You can call out if you need anything."
  - **LIVING EXHIBIT:** Leave the door cracked. Just... don't lock it.
- **[Ask what name to use]** "What should I call you?"
  - **LIVING EXHIBIT:** The doctors called me 'Subject Four.' Don't call me that. Just... don't call me anything yet.

**PLAYER:** All right.

## M-03 - Separate evidence wings

**Placement:** Optional investigations in the Cognitive Wing and Frame Conversion Wing. Use only evidence the player actually finds.
**Knowledge:** A wing adds detail to its own program. It does not prove a specific person's guilt without corroboration.

### Cognitive Wing

**CLEO:** This log documents an invasive neural transfer. Look at the margin notes... the subject's tremors were dismissed as 'calibration noise.'

**PLAYER:** The next entry just says "nonviable."

**CLEO:** A cold word for a pulse stopping. They wrote it with the same pencil they used to log battery levels.

**PLAYER:** Could the survivor tell us what happened?

**CLEO:** We don't interrogate someone who barely survived an ordeal just to fill a footnote in an index. If they never speak of it, the silence stands.

### Frame Conversion Wing

**GAGE:** Heavy pneumatic armature. Reinforced titanium cage bolted straight into thoracic plating.

**PLAYER:** Does this show who performed it?

**GAGE:** The assembly stamps are from the 4th Heavy Field Division. That tells me which workshop machined the pins, not whose hands forced the clamps shut.

**PLAYER:** The report calls the subject a transfer.

**GAGE:** 'Material transfer.' Bastion bureaucrats hiding behind inventory jargon so they didn't have to look at the blood on the floor. Makes my coolant run cold.

**PLAYER:** Do you know whether the researchers were radicals?

**GAGE:** No. Nothing here tells us. "Readiness" doesn't tell us which current they belonged to.

## M-04 - After the discovery

**Placement:** A quiet conversation after the player understands the programs were abusive. Include only characters who have seen or been told the relevant evidence.

**CLEO:** I spent years believing that as long as a record was complete, it was just. I told myself that indexing their names was a way of keeping them safe.

**PLAYER:** You were cataloging what you found.

**CLEO:** I was treating their agony like museum property. Sitting in that observation gallery... I realized I was just filing their screams under neat categorical headers.

**GAGE:** My whole life, I judged a frame by its hydraulic pressure and tensile rating. You take a damaged unit, you weld the cracks, you reinforce the struts, and you get it back on the line. I never asked... what if the frame is a prison? What if the steel is what keeps the wound from ever healing?

**PLAYER:** What happens now?

**GAGE:** We leave our tools on the porch and step back fifty yards. If the clank of my knee actuators makes their hands shake, I clear out of the valley entirely.

**CLEO:** And my stylus stays capped. The Archive can wait in the dark until a human being decides they want their story told.

**PLAYER:** What do we tell the others?

**GAGE:** What the steel shows. Two rogue programs run by people who hid behind faction banners. The engineers who turned those wrenches answer for it. Not every repair sapper trying to keep a town pump running.

**CLEO:** And the Index will have to face what was done under its seal, without excuses.
