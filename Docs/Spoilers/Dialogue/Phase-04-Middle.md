# Phase 4 - Middle: The trials become undeniable

> **Spoilers.** The institute establishes the abusive Continuance Trials without making either faction collectively guilty or turning a survivor into evidence.

## M-01 - The institute

**Placement:** The main story reaches the old research institute. The player finds corroborating records from separate programs.
**Knowledge:** The records establish that the Index's cognitive integrations and Iron Creed frame conversions happened. They do not establish one shared command, every member's knowledge, or every individual's responsibility.

**PLAYER:** This isn't just a stack of damaged clinic files. There are intake forms, procedure logs, and people marked "transferred" with no destination.

**CLEO:** The dates line up with what I found. That's not a clerical error. But we don't know what happened to each person.

**GAGE:** The other wing has frame-conversion notes. The equipment request bears the Creed's name. That tells us who supplied it, not who in the network knew.

**PLAYER:** They did all of this here?

**CLEO:** The records put both projects here. They were separate programs.

**GAGE:** They called it preparation. Some people were misled. Others weren't allowed to leave. That isn't consent.

**PLAYER:** Then there's no softer way to put it. They hurt people.

**CLEO:** Yes.

**GAGE:** We still need to know who did what before we name anyone.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Prioritize finding the person]** "Can we reach the person being kept here safely?"
  - **GAGE:** First we check the route and the door. We don't rush into a room we haven't cleared.
- **[Ask what the records establish]** "Let's separate what we know from what we suspect."
  - **CLEO:** We can do that. The programs existed. People were harmed. We do not yet know who ordered each action or who wants to speak now.
- **[Ask for a pause]** "I need a moment before we go further."
  - **CLEO:** Take it. The evidence is not going anywhere.

## M-02 - First contact with the living exhibit

**Placement:** The player reaches the person displayed as the Index's "living exhibit." Keep the scene calm and allow distance.
**Knowledge:** Their identity, condition, history and wishes are not established by this discovery. The player can learn the display is not voluntary without demanding testimony.

**PLAYER:** I won't ask you to explain anything. Do you want me to stay back?

**LIVING EXHIBIT:** Are you here to move me somewhere else?

**PLAYER:** No. I won't touch you or take you anywhere. Do you want us to leave?

**LIVING EXHIBIT:** I don't know. The door's open, but they put me here. I didn't agree to be shown to people.

**PLAYER:** I'm sorry. We can stay back, leave, or give you space. You don't have to decide yet.

**LIVING EXHIBIT:** Who else is with you?

**PLAYER:** Cleo and Gage are outside. They won't come in unless you ask.

**PLAYER - CHOICES**

- **[Offer immediate practical help]** "Would water, medical help, or a quieter room help right now?"
  - **LIVING EXHIBIT:** Water. Leave it where I can reach it. No one touches me.
- **[Offer privacy]** "I can leave and come back only if you ask."
  - **LIVING EXHIBIT:** Leave the door open. I'll decide whether to call.
- **[Ask what name to use]** "What would you like me to call you?"
  - **LIVING EXHIBIT:** Not yet. You can call me nothing for now.

**PLAYER:** All right.

## M-03 - Separate evidence wings

**Placement:** Optional investigations in the Cognitive Wing and Frame Conversion Wing. Use only evidence the player actually finds.
**Knowledge:** A wing adds detail to its own program. It does not prove a specific person's guilt without corroboration.

### Cognitive Wing

**CLEO:** This log shows the procedure happened. It doesn't say whether the person agreed to it.

**PLAYER:** The next entry says "nonviable."

**CLEO:** We can't know what happened from that word alone.

**PLAYER:** Could the person here confirm it?

**CLEO:** They don't have to explain a file before we offer care.

### Frame Conversion Wing

**GAGE:** This is the frame-conversion work. The design notes describe a protected life-support housing and a mechanical frame.

**PLAYER:** Does this show who performed it?

**GAGE:** There are signatures. That tells us who signed, not what each person did or knew.

**PLAYER:** The report calls the subject a transfer.

**GAGE:** They call the person "the subject" all the way through. Let's not do the same in our notes.

**PLAYER:** Do you know whether the researchers were radicals?

**GAGE:** No. Nothing here tells us. "Readiness" doesn't tell us which current they belonged to.

## M-04 - After the discovery

**Placement:** A quiet conversation after the player understands the programs were abusive. Include only characters who have seen or been told the relevant evidence.

**CLEO:** I thought keeping the files meant I was protecting the people in them.

**PLAYER:** Did you ask them?

**CLEO:** No. I kept asking what the record needed. I never asked what they wanted.

**GAGE:** They called it readiness. I know how to keep a frame working. I never asked what it meant to the person inside one.

**PLAYER:** What happens now?

**GAGE:** We ask what they want. If they don't want me there, I stay away.

**CLEO:** And I won't put their account in my archive unless they ask.

**PLAYER:** What do we tell the others?

**GAGE:** What the records show: two separate programs. The people responsible answer for what they did. A faction name isn't proof.

**CLEO:** These records don't tell us what the rest of the Index knew.
