# Phase 1 — Opening: Waking and making a home

> **Spoilers.** Draft scenes for waking, early farm life, Wellspring and the first family traces.

## O-01 — Waking

**Placement:** The sleeper wakes in the failing pod beneath the farm.
**Knowledge:** The sleeper knows only that the pod malfunctioned. Hob knows the order and that the all-clear never came.

**HOB:** Easy. Don't stand yet. Your legs have been asleep longer than you have.

**PLAYER:** How long?

**HOB:** I don't know the exact count. The pod waited for the all-clear. It never came.

**PLAYER:** What happened?

**HOB:** The war lasted four days. They called it the Four Days. It ended about seventy years ago.

**PLAYER:** Seventy years?

**HOB:** Yes. I'm sorry. There's water, a blanket, and a chair that's nearly level.

**PLAYER — CHOICES**

- **[Ask about the family]** "Where are my parents? The rest of my family?"
  - **HOB:** I don't know enough to answer. I remember them at the farm. After that... I have gaps.
- **[Ask about the pod]** "Can it be fixed?"
  - **HOB:** Not safely today. I kept it running until it woke you. That was the order.
- **[Ask for a moment]** "I need a minute."
  - **HOB:** Of course. I'll stay by the door. You can ask me to leave if you want quiet.

**HOB:** The farm is still here. We can start with one thing at a time.

## O-02 — First work

**Placement:** The sleeper steps into the overgrown garden. Hob introduces a simple farm task.
**Knowledge:** Hob knows the small farm system, not the town's interconnected utilities.

**HOB:** The east bed still drains. The west one holds water for too long. We can start with the east.

**PLAYER:** You kept all this going?

**HOB:** Enough of it. I chose the pod and the garden over repairs I couldn't finish. The town's systems needed more hands than I had.

**PLAYER:** You shouldn't have had to do it alone.

**HOB:** The order kept the farm going. It didn't tell me what to do afterward.

**PLAYER:** You don't have to decide today.

**HOB:** Thank you. That helps.

**PLAYER — CHOICES**

- **[Invite Hob's opinion]** "What would you like to work on?"
  - **HOB:** I would like to clear the east bed with you. If that's all right.
- **[Offer help without assuming]** "You can show me what needs doing. We can decide the rest together."
  - **HOB:** That sounds better than another list of orders.
- **[Focus on the farm]** "Let's get one bed planted first."
  - **HOB:** Yes. One bed is a good size for today.

**HOB:** I can show you the tools. You decide what to plant.

## O-03 — Wellspring

**Placement:** First visit to the town's public commons.
**Speakers:** PLAYER, TOWN ADMINISTRATOR, KEEPER OPERATOR.
**Knowledge:** The town is independently governed. The Administrator can explain the assembly's decisions but does not decide for it.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** Hob told us you were awake. The assembly hasn't settled what that means for the town.

**PLAYER:** I wasn't expecting a vote.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** It wasn't a vote. I meant to welcome you. I'm still learning how not to make everything sound like a meeting.

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** Water's the urgent thing. The pump is holding today, but the intake has been catching again.

**PLAYER:** Can I help?

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** You can help us carry the parts once we know which ones fit. The operators handle the pump.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** And the assembly decides what Wellspring spends on it. If you have an opinion, you can give it. You do not have to take responsibility for the whole town.

**PLAYER — CHOICES**

- **[Ask what they need]** "What would make tomorrow easier?"
  - **KEEPER OPERATOR:** A clean handoff between shifts. The trouble doesn't stop when one of us clocks out.
- **[Offer practical help]** "I can carry things or take notes."
  - **KEEPER OPERATOR:** Notes would help, if you write down what you saw and leave the diagnosis to us.
- **[Set a boundary]** "I need to get used to being awake before I take on town problems."
  - **TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** Sensible. Wellspring was here before you woke. It will still be here while you decide.

## O-04 — The pump needs more than one role

**Placement:** A routine service failure reveals that separate town duties do not cover the whole problem.
**Speakers:** PLAYER, KEEPER OPERATOR, TOWN ADMINISTRATOR, HOB.
**Knowledge:** The failure establishes a coordination gap, not incompetence or a faction-wide flaw.

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** The intake report says the screen is clear. The pressure log says flow dropped before the screen was checked.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** Both shifts completed their assigned checks.

**PLAYER:** Could the readings have been taken at different times?

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** They were. The handoff falls between shifts. No one's checking that the two entries line up.

**HOB:** On the farm, I leave a note when a task is unfinished. It does not repair the task, but the next person knows where to start.

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** We could try a shared log. Operators only. The pump details aren't for a public noticeboard.

**PLAYER:** I can help compare the entries, if you want.

**KEEPER OPERATOR:** Yes. You ask before reaching for a valve. That's a good start.

**TOWN ADMINISTRATOR:** I'll take the proposal to the assembly. It can decide whether the town wants a continuing practice.

**HOB:** A useful idea is still only an idea until people choose to keep using it.

## O-05 — First family trace

**Placement:** The sleeper finds an ordinary note or photograph while restoring the farmhouse.
**Knowledge:** The item supports the parents' presence above ground. It does not settle their fate or explain the missing pod.

**PLAYER:** I remember this table. Not this mark on it.

**HOB:** The mark is mine. I set a kettle down too hard.

**PLAYER:** Were they sitting here?

**HOB:** I think so. I remember your mother laughing. I can't remember what she said.

**PLAYER:** Were they alive after I went under?

**HOB:** I remember them here after you went under. I know they're gone now. I don't remember when that changed, or what happened.

**PLAYER:** I wish you remembered more.

**HOB:** So do I. I won't fill the gap with a guess.

**PLAYER — CHOICES**

- **[Keep the object]** "Let's put it somewhere safe."
  - **HOB:** I'll wrap it. You can decide later what to do with it.
- **[Leave it in place]** "I want it to stay where they left it."
  - **HOB:** All right. I'll keep the area clear.
- **[Ask to stop]** "Not today."
  - **HOB:** Then not today.

## O-06 — A home, not a mission

**Placement:** End of the opening stretch, after the player understands the farm and has met Wellspring residents.
**Knowledge:** The sleeper knows time has passed, Hob's order is fulfilled, and the town's services depend on cooperation. No trial program is implied.

**PLAYER:** Everyone here has a job for me.

**HOB:** Some people have requests. Some have questions. Those aren't the same as orders.

**PLAYER:** I don't know what I'm meant to do next.

**HOB:** You don't have to know tonight.

**PLAYER:** What will you do?

**HOB:** I haven't decided. I might stay and help here. I might see what the town needs. I would like to choose after I've had a little time.

**PLAYER:** Then take the time.

**HOB:** Thank you. That is a kind answer.

**PLAYER:** Tomorrow, we can start with the garden.

**HOB:** If you still want to. For tonight, the house is warm, and the door locks.
