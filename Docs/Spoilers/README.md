# Spoilers

The documents in this folder reveal the story, its secrets and the characters' backstories. Skip them if you want to discover the game by playing it.

- [Lore.md](Lore.md): the world's history, the factions, the characters, the money and the companies, and the tone guide for writers (section 15).
- [StoryTimeline.md](StoryTimeline.md): the provisional story phases, reveal pacing and character knowledge guardrails.
- [StoryFlow/](StoryFlow/README.md): the story-flow overview and phase diagrams.
- [LoreAudit.md](LoreAudit.md): a critical review of lore coherence, characters, tone and writer-facing gaps.
- [Devlog.md](Devlog.md): the rules for the short in-world pieces that end some devlog posts, and the list of published pieces.
- [Characters/Engineer.md](Characters/Engineer.md): the engineer's character sheet.
- [Characters/Hob.md](Characters/Hob.md): Hob's character sheet.
- [Characters/Archivist.md](Characters/Archivist.md): the Index archivist's character sheet.
- [Characters/Nomad.md](Characters/Nomad.md): the nomad's character sheet.
- [Characters/ClinicRobot.md](Characters/ClinicRobot.md): Sal, the Wellspring clinic robot, and the clinic register clue.
- [Characters/Alice.md](Characters/Alice.md): Alice's pre-war character concept and potential human romance arc in the planned release roster.
- [Characters/Reed.md](Characters/Reed.md): a provisional human romance character who blends into robot society through social presentation.
- [Characters/AuthorityIntermediary.md](Characters/AuthorityIntermediary.md): Morrow, a human-origin covert recruiter with a fully mechanical interior and a convincing human exterior.
- [Items/Contamination.md](Items/Contamination.md): names and descriptions for the cleaning crop and the mutated crops, and the lore of the farm's contaminated rows.
- [Dialogue/README.md](Dialogue/README.md): voice-ready draft scenes organized by story phase.
- [Dialogue/Hob-Everyday.md](Dialogue/Hob-Everyday.md): Hob's first meeting in the slice, daily greetings and repeat lines, Ready for the game.
- [Dialogue/Hob-Trust.md](Dialogue/Hob-Trust.md): Hob's trust track in the slice (farm topics, the three step talks, warmer greetings, watered mornings, passing lines on farm events), Ready for the game.
- [Factions/IronCreed.md](Factions/IronCreed.md): the Iron Creed's beliefs, symbol and story direction.
- [Factions/Index.md](Factions/Index.md): the Index's beliefs, symbol and story direction.
- [Factions/Keepers.md](Factions/Keepers.md): the Keepers' beliefs, symbol and story direction.
- [Factions/ContinuanceAuthority.md](Factions/ContinuanceAuthority.md): the Continuance Authority's concealed rule, claim to power and slow-discovery story direction.

Contributors writing dialogue, item descriptions, quests or other story content need these documents. The `vestige-writer` agent (see [.claude/README.md](../../.claude/README.md)) writes and keeps them consistent for the maintainer, and keeps their secrets out of every other document. Everything else (how the game plays, the code, the roadmap) is in the parent [Docs](..) folder and is spoiler-free.
