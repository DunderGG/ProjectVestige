# Devlog in-world pieces

> **Spoilers.** This names the voice behind the devlog's postcards and what each piece commits to canon. The devlog itself never does.

Some devlog posts, published at the separate devlog site, end with a short, clearly fictional piece from inside the world. @DunderGG decided this on 2026-11-27 ([Lore.md, section 13](Lore.md#13-decision-log)). For now the pieces are unnamed postcards; other in-world voices are allowed for variation, and radio transmissions come later, once the game has voices.

## The postcard voice

The postcards are written by Morrow, the Authority's intermediary ([character sheet](Characters/AuthorityIntermediary.md)), at a time before they learn anything about what the Authority is doing. They are never named in public, and the reader is never meant to work out who writes.

## Spoiler rules

These hold for every piece, whatever the voice.

- **No faction.** Never name the Authority or use its vocabulary (assignment, program, service roles, the Continuance as a mandate, its seal).
- **Settled facts only.** A piece may hint at a fact about the person only when it is settled in this folder, and at the world no deeper than the early chapter: what the player can know in Phases 1 and 2 of [StoryTimeline.md](StoryTimeline.md) before "First irregularities" (ordinary life, weather, haze, farming, towns). Never the war's causes, the trials or the family's fate.
- **Never the body.** No hint of the mechanical body, the unchanging face or the conversion.
- **No identity.** No name, pronoun, place or past.
- **No invitation.** No call to action, no "come find me", no address, nothing the reader is asked to do.
- **No knowing wink.** Nothing that tells the reader a secret is there to find.

## Form

- 3 to 6 short lines, about 70 words.
- Ends with a stamp line: a 24-hour time and the weather ("18:20. Haze thinning.").
- Echoes its post: the piece picks up the post's subject, mood or a detail of it, in the world's terms.

## Frequency

About one post in four, irregular. Never two posts in a row. Never announced or labelled as a series.

## Other voices

Old ads, notices, signs and other characters may write a piece for variation, under the same spoiler rules. Radio transmissions come later, once the game has voices.

## Who writes and approves

The devlog editor never writes these pieces. The writer (`vestige-writer`) writes each one, and @DunderGG approves each piece before it is published.

Before publishing, check:

1. Every spoiler rule above.
2. Lore consistency: each fact hinted is settled in this folder and matches the decision log.
3. Length (3 to 6 lines, about 70 words) and the stamp line.
4. The previous post didn't end with a piece.
5. @DunderGG's approval of this piece.

Then add the piece's row to the table below.

The public-side text in the devlog repository (its `CLAUDE.md` and `PUBLISHING.md`) is spoiler-free: it says such pieces exist and who writes and approves them, and names no character or faction.
