# 4. Time, days and seasons

One of the [system guides](../Systems.md); read [How to use this document](../Systems.md#how-to-use-this-document) first.

**Status:** day and clock built (`UVestigeClockSubsystem`, a game-instance subsystem, with `UVestigeClockSettings`; 2026-11-04), and the lighting that follows it (`AVestigeSkyLighting`). The calendar (seasons of 28 days, the date and the days left, [Roadmap.md, 4.2](../Roadmap.md#42-farming-depth)) is built (2026-11-14, below). Weather is milestone 3.

**References:** no single Epic system; the rules here follow [Architecture.md, sections 15 and 18](../Architecture.md#15-the-day-flow). Lighting follows Epic's "Sky Atmosphere", "Exponential Height Fog" and "Lumen" pages. The calendar: Lyra has none (its source has no season or calendar); Epic's "Text Formatting" page for plural forms; Lyra's `ULyraGameData` for global data in one primary data asset, as `UVestigeGameData` already is.

**Rules:**

- One owner of game time (`UVestigeClockSubsystem`), in the game instance so it keeps time across maps; its speed and hours, sunrise and sunset included, are project settings (`UVestigeClockSettings`), never a level's. Everything else reads it or listens to its events (`OnNewDay`, `OnNewDayFinished`, `OnHourStarted`, `OnMinuteStarted`, `OnDayEnded`). Nothing keeps its own clock: something that happens at a time of day listens to `OnHourStarted`, which carries the hour since the start of the day's midnight (22 for 22:00, 24 for the next midnight), and something that goes on steadily over game time (the late-night drain) listens to `OnMinuteStarted`, which carries the minutes since that midnight (1441 for 00:01), rather than watching the clock or running a timer of its own. Both come once per whole minute or hour, in order, even when a long frame or a skip passes several, the minute before its hour.
- The game mode (`AVestigeGameMode`) owns the end of the day. The clock says when a day has ended (`OnDayEnded`: it reached the day's end, or something such as going to bed asked for it with `EndDay`) and stops; the game mode starts the next day. Whatever reacts to how the night went (the rest component's exhaustion) does it on `OnNewDay`, reading the clock's `GetLastDayEnd`, so the save made after it holds the result. Nothing but the game mode starts a day from `OnDayEnded`: a listener after it may already be in the new day.
- Blueprints, and so mods, hear of a new day through `On New Day` (`OnNewDayBlueprint`), the extension point broadcast after `OnNewDay` and before the save ([Architecture.md, section 12](../Architecture.md#extension-points-today)). A Blueprint finds the clock with **Get Vestige Clock**.
- Game time is separate from real time and from `UWorld::GetTimeSeconds`. Pausing and menus stop game time: the clock ticks with the open world, so pause stops it and time dilation (`slomo`) speeds it.
- Daily changes happen in `OnNewDay` listeners that finish synchronously ([Architecture.md, section 15](../Architecture.md#15-the-day-flow)).
- **The calendar is the day count.** The clock's day count (from 1, saved under `Base.Clock`) is the only calendar state. The date (year, season, day of the season, days left) is worked out from it by the calendar's rule (`FVestigeCalendar::GetDate`), so it is never saved, and nothing keeps a season or a day of the season of its own: ask the clock's `GetDate`. A new game starts on the first day of the calendar's first season.
- **The calendar is data.** Seasons are gameplay tags under `Vestige.Season` (the crops' `Seasons` name them). Their order, display names and the days in a season are the game data's `Calendar` (`DA_GameData`), which the clock copies once in `Initialize`. Code never names a season: "the first season" is the calendar's first.
- **Days left are the days after today:** the days in a season minus the day of the season, so 0 on the last day. A crop planted today ripens in its season when its days to mature are no more than the days left; the seeds' warning compares exactly these. The player hears the days left in the morning's day message; the HUD shows the season and the day of the season only.
- **A season starts on a day event.** `StartNextDay` broadcasts `OnSeasonStarted(Date)` when the calendar says the new day starts a new season (`GetDayKind` is `NewSeason`: the first day of a season after a day of another season), after the day count moves and before `OnNewDay`, with `OnNewDay`'s contract (finish every change to saved state in the handler). It isn't broadcast for a new game, a restore or a developer jump, nor by a calendar without seasons or of one season. Season rules (crops that wither) listen to it. The player hears of it in the morning's one day message, whose tag the game mode picks from the same `GetDayKind` ([section 15](15-EffectsAndFeedback.md)), so the player is told by the rule the season rules follow.
- Weather is chosen once per day from data (per-season weights), so it's saved as a value, not re-rolled on load.
- Lighting, fog and sky react to time and weather through the sky lighting actor (`AVestigeSkyLighting`, one per outdoor level), which reads the clock and its sunrise and sunset hours and never changes them; nothing else moves the sun. The sun turns in small steps, not every frame, so virtual shadow maps keep its cached shadows between them; brightness and colour may change every frame.

**Pitfalls:**

- Timers (`FTimerManager`) and periodic gameplay effects for game-time events: they run on real time, ignore skips and a stopped clock, and don't save. Schedule game-time events against the clock's events.
- Comparing hours as floats for equality; compare against ranges or minute counts.
- A season counter kept by a listener (incremented on `OnNewDay`): it drifts from the clock after a load or a developer jump. Read `GetDate`.
- Changing the calendar's data (the days in a season, the seasons' order) moves the date of every existing save, since a save keeps only the day count. Before the first public build that is fine; after it, it needs a migration ([Architecture.md, section 6](../Architecture.md#6-saving)).
- A season's name written in code: names are the calendar's `FText`, so they translate and the HUD and the messages agree.
- A calendar of one season (the "keep spring" option of [GameDesign.md, section 15](../GameDesign.md)) starts that season again every N days, and a season message would read "Spring is over, and spring has begun.": `GetDayKind` gives `NewSeason` only when the day before was another season, so that morning gets the usual day message and no `OnSeasonStarted`.
- Listeners of `OnSeasonStarted` run in no guaranteed order (a multicast delegate's): a season rule whose messages must come in an order (withering after the season message) orders them itself.

**The calendar** (2026-11-14, [Roadmap.md, 4.2](../Roadmap.md#42-farming-depth); the player's side is [GameDesign.md, section 5](../GameDesign.md#5-time-days-and-seasons)).

*Take, skip, change:* take Lyra's one global data asset (ours is `UVestigeGameData`, already loaded once at startup and cooked as the primary asset type `VestigeGameData`, [section 9](09-AbilitySystem.md)) for the calendar's definition. Skip a calendar subsystem or a season actor: the day count is the clock's, and a second owner would keep a second count. Change [Architecture.md, section 18](../Architecture.md#18-expected-changes)'s "seasons belong to the game state": a game state dies with each map, the clock lives in the game instance.

*Types:*

- **`FVestigeSeason`** (`USTRUCT(BlueprintType)`, `Content/VestigeCalendar.h`): `FGameplayTag Tag` (`meta = (Categories = "Vestige.Season")`), `FText DisplayName` ("Spring", the name at the start of a sentence and on the HUD) and `FText NameInSentence` ("spring", the name in the middle of a sentence), both translated as asset text. Code never changes a name's case (`ToLower` breaks languages that capitalise nouns, and translators can't fix it).
- **`FVestigeCalendar`** (`USTRUCT(BlueprintType)`, same header), the `Calendar` property of `UVestigeGameData` (`EditDefaultsOnly`, category **Calendar**): `int32 DaysPerSeason = 28` (`ClampMin = 1`) and `TArray<FVestigeSeason> Seasons` (`TitleProperty` the name), both private with getters (`GetDaysPerSeason`, at least 1, and `GetSeasons`). Its rules, all `const` and pure:
   - `FVestigeDate GetDate(int32 Day) const`: with `Index = Max(Day, 1) - 1`, the day of the season is `Index % DaysPerSeason + 1`, the season `(Index / DaysPerSeason) % Seasons.Num()`, the year `Index / (DaysPerSeason * Seasons.Num()) + 1`, and the days left `DaysPerSeason - DayOfSeason`. With no seasons the date has no season (index `INDEX_NONE`, an empty tag) and year 1, and still counts the day of the season and the days left.
   - `const FVestigeSeason* FindSeason(const FGameplayTag&) const`, `nullptr` for a tag the calendar hasn't, and `FText GetSeasonName(const FVestigeDate&) const`, the date's season's `DisplayName` (empty without one), which the HUD and the report values use.
   - `FFormatNamedArguments GetReportValues(int32 Day) const`: the values a feedback report about that day gives: `Day` (the day count), `Season` (its season's `DisplayName`, empty without one), `SeasonInSentence` (its `NameInSentence`), `DayOfSeason` and `DaysLeft` (a number). One function, so every report names the date the same way. No value is a finished phrase ("25 days left", "last day"): the text is the responses' ([section 15](15-EffectsAndFeedback.md)), and a last day gets a report of its own.
   - `EVestigeDayKind GetDayKind(int32 Day) const` (`enum class EVestigeDayKind : uint8 { Usual, NewSeason, LastDay }`, a fixed set of calendar states, so an enum, [Architecture.md, section 12](../Architecture.md#rules-from-now-on), rule 2): `NewSeason` when `Day` is a season's first day and the day before was another season (never day 1, a calendar without seasons or one of one season), else `LastDay` when `Day` has no days left, else `Usual`. New season comes first; the two meet only in a 1-day season. The one rule for which morning it is: the clock's `OnSeasonStarted`, the game mode's day tag and the save subsystem's loaded tag all follow it.
   - `FFormatNamedArguments GetSeasonStartValues(int32 Day) const`: `GetReportValues(Day)` plus `PreviousSeason`, the `DisplayName` of the season of `Day - 1` (for day 1, the day's own season, so the value always exists).
   - `BlueprintType` on both structs only so editor scripts can make and set them, as on `FFarmCropGrowthStage`; their properties are `EditDefaultsOnly` only, and Blueprints don't read them.
   - `#if WITH_EDITOR` `EDataValidationResult Validate(FDataValidationContext&) const`, called from `UVestigeGameData::IsDataValid`: one error each for no seasons, a `DaysPerSeason` below 1 (`ClampMin` only limits the Details panel, and the getter's floor of 1 would hide it), a tag that is empty, is the parent `Vestige.Season` itself or isn't under it (the native parent tag `VestigeGameplayTags::Season`, since `Categories` only filters the picker; `MatchesTag` is also true for the parent, so that case is checked apart), a tag used twice, an empty `DisplayName` and an empty `NameInSentence`.
   - `#if WITH_DEV_AUTOMATION_TESTS`: `static FVestigeCalendar MakeForTests(int32 DaysPerSeason, TArray<FVestigeSeason> Seasons)`, and `UVestigeGameData::SetCalendarForTests(const FVestigeCalendar&)` beside `SetEffectsForTests`.
- **`FVestigeDate`** (plain struct, same header, like `FVestigeDayEnd`): `Day`, `Year`, `SeasonIndex`, `Season` (tag), `DayOfSeason`, `DaysLeft`, `IsFirstDayOfSeason()`, `IsLastDayOfSeason()` (no days left) and `HasSeason()` (false when the calendar has no seasons).
- **`DA_GameData`** holds spring, summer, autumn and winter (`Vestige.Season.Spring` and so on, names "Spring", "Summer", "Autumn", "Winter" and in a sentence "spring", "summer", "autumn", "winter") and 28 days. The four season tags stay in `DefaultGameplayTags.ini`, since no code names them; only their parent `Vestige.Season` becomes native.

*The clock:* copies `UVestigeGameData::Get().GetCalendar()` in `Initialize` (the Asset Manager loads the game data in `UEngine::Init`, before any game instance exists) and logs an error (`LogVestigeTime`) when it has no seasons. `const FVestigeCalendar& GetCalendar() const`; `FVestigeDate GetDate() const` (today's); `FOnVestigeSeasonStarted OnSeasonStarted` (native, `const FVestigeDate&`), broadcast as the rules say, and logged at `Log` ("Summer began on day 29"). No Blueprint twin and no Blueprint getter yet: like the rest of the clock's state, they come when a mod or content Blueprint first needs them ([Architecture.md, section 12](../Architecture.md#extension-points-today)); a Blueprint can already react on `On New Day`. `bool JumpToDay(int32 Day)`, for developer commands: sets the day count and keeps the time, broadcasting nothing, as a restore does, so the days between don't happen (no crop grows) and the next new day saves the new count; `false`, changing nothing, for a day before 1.

*Saving:* nothing new. `Base.Clock` keeps its `Day` and its version 1, and the date comes back with the day.

*Who uses it:* the HUD's status line shows today's season and day ([section 6](06-UserInterface.md)); the game mode reports the date with the day's feedback, its tag's child form chosen by `GetDayKind`, with `GetSeasonStartValues` for a new season ([section 15](15-EffectsAndFeedback.md)); the save subsystem reports the loaded date, its last-day form chosen by `GetDayKind` too (a one-line mapping: the subsystem builds the report privately, so it has no test until the hook in [Roadmap.md, 4.2](../Roadmap.md#42-farming-depth) exists, and the rule is tested in `GetDayKind`); the `SetDay` console command calls `JumpToDay` ([section 19](19-DebuggingAndCheats.md)). Next on the roadmap: the seeds' warning reads `GetDate().DaysLeft`, and withering compares a crop's `Seasons` with the date's season on `OnSeasonStarted`.

*Cost:* the date is a few integer operations, worked out when something asks; the HUD builds its date text once a day. Nothing runs per frame or per hour that didn't before.

*Tests:* `Vestige.Calendar.*` on made-up calendars (day 1 is the first season's first day; the days left are the days after today, 27 on day 1 and 0 on day 28; the next season starts after the last day; a new year after the last season; a day before 1 counts as day 1; a calendar without seasons still counts days; a calendar with no days counts one day a season; finding a season by tag; the report values name the season, and the season-start values the previous one and the new one in a sentence; the kind of day: a new season only when the season changes, the last day, a new season before the last day in a 1-day season, and usual days otherwise), `Vestige.GameData.TheCalendarHasFourSeasonsOf28Days` (the one test that names 28 and the four tags) and the invalid calendars in data validation, and `Vestige.Clock.*` and `Vestige.GameMode.*` with the game's calendar, their expected days and names taken from `GetCalendar()` (the last day is `GetDaysPerSeason()`, the next season `GetSeasons()[1]`), so tuning the data breaks only the game data test (the date follows the day count and comes back from a save; a season starts once, on its first morning, before `OnNewDay`; not on other days, a new game, a restore or a jump; a jump keeps the time and broadcasts nothing).

*Known gap:* a mod can add or rename a season only by replacing `DA_GameData`; merging calendars from several sources comes with mod loading ([Architecture.md, section 12](../Architecture.md#12-designing-for-mods)), as for the feedback responses ([section 15](15-EffectsAndFeedback.md)).
