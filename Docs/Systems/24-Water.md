# 24. Water sources and the watering can

One of the [system guides](../Systems.md); read [How to use this document](../Systems.md#how-to-use-this-document) first.

**Status:** designed 2026-11-20 for [RoadmapArchive.md, 4.2](../RoadmapArchive.md#42-farming-depth), "Clean water as a resource", on the game designer's recommended options ([GameDesign.md, section 6](../GameDesign.md#water) and section 15, "How much clean water there is"), until @DunderGG answers them; built and tested 2026-11-20 as designed (the C++, the data made by `EditorScripts/SetUpWell.py`: `DA_Item_WateringCan`'s fragment, the five feedback rows, `BP_Well` and its place in `L_Farm`, and the tests below). Tile water itself (the level from 0 to 2 on each tile) is [section 22](22-FarmFields.md)'s. Dirty water, rain collectors, purifiers and the sprinkler build on this guide later.

**References:**

- Lyra's inventory (`Source/LyraGame/Inventory`): `ULyraInventoryItemInstance::StatTags`, an `FGameplayTagStackContainer` (a gameplay tag and a count per entry, with `AddStack`, `RemoveStack` and `GetStackCount`, which returns 0 for an absent tag), and `UInventoryFragment_SetStats`, a fragment whose `InitialItemStats` (`TMap<FGameplayTag, int32>`) it writes into a new instance in `OnInstanceCreated`. Lyra's weapons keep their ammunition that way (`Lyra.ShooterGame.Weapon.MagazineAmmo`). This is the pattern for the water in a can: state that belongs to one item the player carries, keyed by a tag.
- Epic's "Developer Settings" page (`UDeveloperSettings`, `Config = Game`, `DefaultConfig`): where the well's daily supply lives, in settings of the water's own, as the tile water numbers live in the fields' ([section 22](22-FarmFields.md)).
- Epic's "Programming Subsystems" page and `FSubsystemCollectionBase::InitializeDependency` in 5.8 (`Subsystems/SubsystemCollection.h`): a game-instance subsystem outlives every map, so the farm's water is there when its map isn't, as the fields are ([section 22](22-FarmFields.md)).
- The interaction guide ([section 7](07-Interaction.md)) for the well as an `IVestigeInteractable`, and the tools-as-abilities rules ([section 9](09-AbilitySystem.md)) for the watering can's ability.

**What we take from Lyra and what we skip:**

- **Take:** per-item state as tag-keyed counts (a stat tag and a value), so the inventory carries the water without knowing what water is, and a second kind of water later is a second tag, not a new field; the fragment that defines what a fresh item holds.
- **Skip:** item instance objects. Our slots are an item ID and a count ([section 8](08-Inventory.md)), so the stats sit on the slot, and only items that don't stack (`MaxStackSize` 1) have them; one `UObject` per carried item is more than one can needs. Skip writing initial stats into a new item (`OnInstanceCreated`): an absent stat reads as the fresh value its fragment defines, which needs no hook in the inventory and loads an older save's can full. Skip the replicated fast array.

**Rules:**

- **The farm's clean water is one store, owned by `UFarmWaterSubsystem`,** a game-instance subsystem in `Farming/`, so the well, the watering can and the sprinkler to come draw on the same water whichever map is open ([GameDesign.md, section 16](../GameDesign.md#16-mechanics-glossary), the water store). The well isn't the store: it is the place the player draws from. Rain collectors and purifiers add to the store later.
- **The store refills by the day, not on an event.** `FFarmWaterStore` holds the day it was last drawn from (`Day`) and how much was drawn that day (`Drawn`). What is left on `Today` is `DailySupply - Drawn` (never below 0) when `Day` is `Today`, and the whole `DailySupply` on any other day, earlier or later (a developer's day jump). Drawing on another day first starts that day at 0 drawn. Nothing carries over (option (a) of the refill question). So the morning refill needs no new-day handler and has no order to keep against other `OnNewDay` listeners: a sprinkler that draws at dawn finds the day's full supply whichever listener runs first. `OnNewDay`'s listener order is no contract (a multicast broadcast calls its listeners from last to first, [section 8](08-Inventory.md), the hand's event), which is why the refill doesn't depend on it.
- **`UFarmWaterSubsystem`'s interface:** `static Get(const UWorld*)`, `GetCleanWaterLeft()` (today's, with the day from `UVestigeClockSubsystem::GetCurrentDay()` and the supply from `UFarmWaterSettings::Get().GetWellWaterPerDay()`), and `DrawCleanWater(int32 Amount)`, which takes up to `Amount` (nothing for 0 or less) and returns how much it took. It reads the supply on every call, so a changed setting applies at once and the save holds no supply. `Initialize` takes the clock and the save registry with `InitializeDependency` and registers as a game owner; `Deinitialize` unregisters. It doesn't subscribe to the clock. No event yet: nothing shows the store's level except the well's prompt, which reads it on the scan; the brass dial (effects, [section 15](15-EffectsAndFeedback.md)) adds `OnCleanWaterChanged`, broadcast on a draw and on `OnNewDay`, when it is built.
- **The numbers are data.** The well's 24 a day is `WellWaterPerDay` on `UFarmWaterSettings` (Project Settings > Game > Vestige Farm Water, `Config/DefaultGame.ini`, `ClampMin` 0; 0 makes a dry well). The getter clamps it to at least 0 at runtime too (`FMath::Max(0, WellWaterPerDay)`), as `UFarmFieldSettings::GetWaterRules` clamps, because `ClampMin` is editor metadata and a hand-edited or modded ini can hold a negative value. They are settings of their own, not the fields', because their owner is the water subsystem and the roadmap brings more of them (rain collectors, the purifier, the dirty source, the sprinkler's draw): moving a number between settings classes later moves its ini section, which is a config migration. The can's 8 is `Capacity` on the can item's `UFarmWaterContainerFragment` (`ClampMin` 1), set on `DA_Item_WateringCan`. One watering pours one water (`FarmWateringCan::WaterPerWatering`, 1): a watering is one step on one tile, and the step's size is the tile rules'.
- **The water in a can is a stat of its inventory slot,** `Vestige.ItemStat.Water.Clean` (a native tag), through the inventory's slot stats ([section 8](08-Inventory.md)). An absent stat is a full can: a can from the starting items, one restored from a save made before this system, or one bought later all start full without the inventory knowing what a can is. Pouring and filling write the value, 0 included. `FarmWateringCan::GetWater(Inventory, Slot)` returns the slot's water, clamped to 0 to `Capacity`, or `INDEX_NONE` when the slot's item has no container fragment.
- **A kind of water later is a second stat tag** (`Vestige.ItemStat.Water.Dirty`), so the slot's layout and the save don't change. The design's rule for then ([GameDesign.md, section 15](../GameDesign.md#15-open-questions), Q7 of 2026-11-20): a can holds one kind at a time, and dirty water poured into clean makes all of it dirty, written as the clean stat set to 0 and the dirty one to the total. Each source names the kind it gives; the dirty source is a prop of its own.
- **`FarmWateringCan`** (`Farming/FarmWateringCan.h`), the can's rules in one place for the ability, the well and the HUD:
  - `GetCapacity(const UVestigeItemData*)`: the container fragment's `Capacity`, or `INDEX_NONE` for an item without one.
  - `GetWater(const UVestigeInventoryComponent&, int32 Slot)`: above.
  - `Pour(UVestigeInventoryComponent&, int32 Slot)`: takes `WaterPerWatering` from the slot's can; false, changing nothing, when it has less or isn't a can.
  - `FindCanToFill(const UVestigeInventoryComponent&)`: the first slot, in slot order, whose can isn't full; else the first slot with a can (a full one); else `INDEX_NONE`. So F fills the can wherever it is in the bag, and with two cans the emptier search never skips a can that needs water.
  - `Fill(UVestigeInventoryComponent&, int32 Slot, int32 Amount)`: adds up to `Amount` and never above `Capacity`, returns how much it added.
- **Watering pours from the can in hand.** `UFarmGameplayAbility_Water` overrides the base's `CanUseToolNow` ([section 9](09-AbilitySystem.md)): the can in the inventory's selected hotbar slot (`GetInventory()`, `GetSelectedHotbarSlot()`) must hold at least one water, or the press says "Your can is empty. Fill it at the well." (`Vestige.Feedback.Water.CanEmpty`) and costs nothing. An item in hand that names the watering can's tool but has no container fragment (a content mistake) is refused the same way, with a warning in `LogFarm`. The base asks `CanUseToolNow` after it has found a target and before `CanUseOn`, so the order of the reasons is the design's: "Stand facing a farm tile." first, then the empty can, then the tile's own ("Nothing to water here.", "The soil is already soaked."). `UseOn` waters the tile through `UFarmField::Water` and then pours with `Pour`; a pour that fails after a watering that worked (nothing runs between them that should change the bag) is logged as a warning and the watering stands.
- **A held E waters until the can is empty, then says so once.** The base reports a `CanUseToolNow` refusal the first time it happens in an activation, on the press or on a repeat, and waits quietly after that, as it waits for a target; a new press reports again ([section 9](09-AbilitySystem.md)). Each tile is still watered once per press (the tile rule).
- **The well, `AFarmWell`** (`Farming/`, as the bed is), is an actor with `WellMesh` (a static mesh component with its default blocking collision; no mesh in C++, `BP_Well` sets `SM_Well`) and `InteractionVolume` (a box with the `VestigeInteractable` profile and overlap events off, as the bed's: half extents 55, 55, 65 cm at 65 cm up, up to the well's 1.3 m). It is sized for `SM_Well`'s ring hull, `UCX_SM_Well_00`: a 12-sided prism with its corners 45 cm from the centre at 15 + 30k degrees (`make_well.py`'s coping, turned to start at 15 degrees), so its flat faces, 43.5 cm out (45 cos 15), face the axes and the 30 and 60 degree directions, and a corner points along each 45-degree diagonal. The farmer's capsule (42 cm) keeps his centre at least 85.5 cm out against a face and 87 cm against a corner. The box reaches 55 cm on the axes (30.5 cm short of the farmer), 63.5 cm at 30 and 60 degrees (22 cm short) and 77.8 cm at its corners on the diagonals (9.2 cm short), and passes the ring by 11.5 cm on the axes. So the facing ray never starts inside it ([section 7](07-Interaction.md), facing), and the scan's 100 cm sphere reaches it from any spot against the ring. The pump's hull (`UCX_SM_Well_01`) stays inside the ring's outline. A change to the ring's radius or sides re-checks these numbers.
- **The well's option.** `GetInteractionOptions(Interactor)` adds one option, `{Text "Fill can ({Left} left)", Action "Fill"}`, with no location (the ray picks the well as it picks the bed), whenever the interactor's inventory (on its player state, as `UFarmingComponent` finds it) holds an item with a container fragment; with no can there is nothing to offer. The prompt then reads "F Fill can (16 left)" (Q4, option (a)), and "(0 left)" when dry: the option stays, so F tells the player why. The text is rebuilt only when `Left` differs from the one it was last built for (a `mutable` cache of the number and the `FText` on the well), since the scan asks five times a second and the rule is no unchanged strings rebuilt on a timer ([CodingGuidelines.md, section 12](../CodingGuidelines.md#12-performance)). The interaction component compares the text each scan, so a new number redraws the prompt.
- **Filling** (`Interact` with the `Fill` action; any other action does nothing). In this order, each a feedback report, so the design's order holds:
  1. `FindCanToFill`; `INDEX_NONE` does nothing (the option wasn't offered).
  2. The can is full: "Your can is already full." (`Water.CanFull`), nothing drawn. The player's reason comes first, whatever the well holds.
  3. The store has nothing left: "The well is dry. It refills overnight." (`Water.WellDry`).
  4. Otherwise it draws what the can lacks (`Capacity - Water`) with `DrawCleanWater`, fills the can with what it got, and says "Can filled." (`Water.CanFilled`, Q3 option (b), until the splash and the sound exist), or "The well ran dry before your can was full." (`Water.WellRanDry`) when it got less.
  Filling is free and instant (Q2, option (a)): no stamina, no clock time, no held ability. The interact ability ends at once as for the bed.
- **The hand label.** `AVestigeHUD::FormatItemInHand` gets the can's water and capacity: for an item with a container fragment it reads "{Item} ({Water}/{Capacity})", "Watering Can (5/8)" ([GameDesign.md, section 6](../GameDesign.md#water)); the bag line stays "Watering Can 1". The HUD reads `FarmWateringCan::GetWater` for the selected slot. A pour or a fill changes a stat, which broadcasts `OnInventoryChanged` but not `OnItemInHandChanged` (the item didn't change), so `RefreshInventoryTexts` also calls `RefreshItemInHandText` when the hand's water differs from the number last shown (`ShownHandWater`, `INDEX_NONE` for no can).
- **Saving.** `Base.FarmWater` is `FFarmWaterSaveData` (`Farming/FarmWaterSaveData.h`): `Day` (`INDEX_NONE` before anything was drawn) and `CleanWaterDrawn`, version 1. No record (a new game, or a save from before this system) is a new game's store: full. The can's water is saved with its slot in `Base.Inventory` ([section 8](08-Inventory.md)); a save from before it has no stats, so the can loads full. Neither raises a version: both are additions whose defaults suit older records ([section 1](01-Saving.md)).
- **Placement.** `BP_Well` (`Content/Vestige/Blueprints`, parent `AFarmWell`, `WellMesh` set to `SM_Well` at scale 1 and location 0) stands in `L_Farm`'s service yard per the [home farm plan](../Levels/HomeFarm/README.md): on the far side of the field from the bed and the Player Start, outside the field's apron, 9 m from the nearest field edge (Q6, option (b)). With the grid at (180, 60) and 8 by 8 tiles of 100 cm, that is X 1880, Y 360, Z 0, yaw 0; the shipping bin goes beside it later. The level has no service yard massing yet, so the well stands on the floor alone; the blockout ([home farm plan](../Levels/HomeFarm/README.md)) may move it, keeping the distance.
- **Layers.** The fragment is in `Content/` with the other fragments and knows no inventory. `FarmWateringCan`, the store, the subsystem and the well are in `Farming/`, which may read `Inventory/`, `Content/` and `Time/`. The HUD (`UI/`) reads `Farming/`. The inventory knows only tags and values.
- **Logging:** `LogFarm` for the well and the store (a draw at `Verbose`; a can without a container at `Warning`), `LogVestigeInventory` for slot stats (refused or dropped stats at `Warning`).
- **Performance:** no tick, no timer, no new-day work. The well's options cost a component lookup and a walk over the bag's 24 slots for a container fragment (stopping at the first) five times a second while the farmer is near it, plus an integer compare; the text only on a change. Pouring and filling are one stat write on a press. Nothing loops over many objects, so no trace scope is needed; add one if a later source loops over placed collectors.
- **Mods:** a mod's item becomes a water container with the fragment and its own `Capacity`. A Blueprint mod's well is a Blueprint subclass of `AFarmWell`, which may change the mesh and the interaction box and keeps the well's rules. A source with rules of its own (a mod's pump, a collector) needs the subsystem's functions and the can's rules (`FarmWateringCan`, C++ only for now) exposed to Blueprints, which happens with the first Blueprint source; until then only a C++ mod can write one ([Architecture.md, section 12](../Architecture.md#12-designing-for-mods)).

**Pitfalls:**

- Refilling the store in an `OnNewDay` handler: its order against a sprinkler's handler would depend on the order of subscription, which a multicast broadcast doesn't keep. Read the day instead.
- Treating a stat of 0 as absent: an empty can would load full. Absent means fresh; 0 is empty, and both are saved as they are.
- Keeping the can's water anywhere but its slot (on the player state, the farming component or the tool data): a second can, a chest or a sold can would then disagree with it.
- Building the prompt's `FText` on every scan.
- Renaming `Vestige.ItemStat.Water.Clean`: its name is in save files ([section 8](08-Inventory.md), slot stats).
- A placed well without `BP_Well`'s mesh: the C++ class has none, so it is invisible.
